DUSKBLOOM CENTER v0.9.1

This is the first distribution-focused Center build.

RUN
1. Extract this folder.
2. Double-click BUILD_DUSKBLOOM_CENTER.bat.
3. Center builds and opens automatically.

INSTALL-ONCE UPDATE MODEL
Center contains the update engine and can consume one permanent HTTPS update_catalog.json.
That feed can publish component updates (Screen/Web/Sonar/etc.) independently and can also publish Center updates.

IMPORTANT
The permanent online feed URL is intentionally not invented or hard-coded yet. Once a real DuskBloom release host/repository is chosen, put its catalog URL into Center Settings > Updates. After that, copies of Center can keep discovering future releases without replacing Center manually.
